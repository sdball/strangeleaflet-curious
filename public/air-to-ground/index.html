    Scope · last 60 min

      Reading the list

      • DNLK Downlink: the crew writes to the ground.
      • UPLK Uplink: a dispatcher writes back. RE points at the message it answers.
      • NOTE ACARS Drama's own commentary.
      • ALRT The message mentions a diversion, a medical problem, an emergency, fire, smoke, fumes, or low fuel.
      • FLT A scrambled tag for one flight. Press it to follow that flight's messages.

      What this is

      ACARS is the text link between an airliner and its airline. Crews type to dispatch and maintenance, dispatchers type back, and it all comes through in capital letters.

      ACARS Drama on Bluesky posts the human ones, with names and numbers blanked out as XXXX. This page reads their public feed every 30 seconds through Bluesky's public API and types each new message as it lands.

      For TV news on the same kind of screen, try Chyron Wire.

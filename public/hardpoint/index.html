The Cabinet of Curios

Hardpoint

You pilot one mech, the Halberd, against a squad of hostiles on a battlefield built at random. Use walls and barricades for cover, keep an eye on your heat, and decide when it's worth pushing your reactor past what's safe. The rules are inspired by the LANCER tabletop game.

Pick an action, then tap a lit hex or a ringed hostile. Tap it again, or press the button that appears, to commit. On a keyboard, the arrow keys move a cursor over the map, Enter picks, and Esc backs out. Each action button shows its key.

    How a turn works

    • You get a move and two quick actions. Move up to your Speed in hexes, and rough ground costs 2. The quick actions are Boost (move again), Skirmish (fire one weapon), Lock On, and Smoke. Or spend both on one full action: Barrage (fire two different weapons) or Stabilize.
    • Attacks roll a d20 against Evasion. You add your pilot's grit. Accuracy and difficulty cancel one for one, and whatever is left becomes d6s: roll them all, keep the highest, and add it for accuracy or subtract it for difficulty. A total of 20 or more is a critical hit, and every damage die rolls twice and keeps the higher.
    • Cover only stops ranged attacks. Scrub and smoke give soft cover, +1 difficulty. A barricade beside the target, or a wall edge that half hides it, gives hard cover, +2. Walls block sight completely, but you can shoot over barricades. Melee attacks ignore cover.
    • Getting close has a cost. Moving next to a hostile engages you both. Your movement stops there, and ranged attacks made while engaged take +1 difficulty. If you start moving while inside a hostile's threat, it gets a free swing at you first (overwatch), and your cleaver does the same to them.
    • HP is only the first layer. At 0 HP the Halberd loses a point of structure and its HP refills. Then you roll a structure check: a d6 for each point lost so far, keeping the lowest. A 5 or 6 leaves you impaired for a turn, 2 to 4 wrecks a weapon or system, and a 1 can stun or destroy you. Lose all four points and the mech is done.
    • Heat is the price of pushing. Overcharge buys an extra quick action for 1 heat, then 1d3, 1d6, and 1d6+4 for each later use in the same battle. The Arc Lance adds 2 heat per shot. Go past your heat cap and you lose a point of stress and roll an overheat check the same way as a structure check. Stabilize clears all heat.
    • Brace when a hit would cost structure. It's a reaction: you take half damage, and attacks against you take +1 difficulty until the end of your next turn. The cost is that next turn you get only one quick action, though the Halberd's Steady Footing still lets you move.
    • Lock On sets up a shot. It costs a quick action and makes no roll. The next attack against that hostile gains +1 accuracy.

    Know the hostiles

    • Pickets are grunts. Any damage destroys one, even a miss with Reliable damage. They hang back behind cover and plink at you with carbines.
    • Hounds are fast and fragile. They run you down with Speed 5 and are hard to hit, but six damage drops one.
    • Brutes hit hard and slowly. A Brute's maul knocks lighter mechs back a hex. Stepping away from one gives it a free swing, so pick your moment.
    • Lobbers shoot over walls. Their mortars arc over anything, so cover doesn't help against them. A Lobber has to spend a turn reloading after each shot, and it can't fire at a target right next to it.

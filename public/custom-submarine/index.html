Custom Submarine

Draw your own submarine for Cozy Submarine, one pixel at a time. Start from the yellow sub or a clear grid, watch your design sail in the shallows and the deep, then send it out to sea.

Click or drag on the grid to paint, and right-click to rub out. On the grid, the arrow keys move a cursor and Space paints. P pencil, F fill, I picker, 0–9 pick a color, L lights, Ctrl Z undo. To redraw the creatures you meet at sea, use Pixel Bench.

The drawing board

Arrow keys move the cursor, Shift moves four at a time. Space or Enter uses the tool, Delete rubs out.

Things to notice

  • Every pixel is a letter. The game keeps its sub as eleven lines of text, one letter per pixel, and a short list saying which color each letter means. A space is empty sea. Copy your sub to see yours written out that way.
  • Art faces right. The game flips it when you turn around, so draw the nose on the right and the stern on the left.
  • The propeller finds its own place. It goes two pixels behind the stern (your leftmost pixels), halfway up them. The grid shows it faintly in the strip on the left. It isn't part of the drawing, so it spins the same for every sub.
  • Each light shines from its lens. The sub has three lights, front, bottom, and rear, and each has its own swatch, with an arrow for the way it shines. Paint a lens where you want that beam to come out. With more than one, the outermost shines: the frontmost, the lowest, the rearmost. With none, the beam leaves from the nose, the middle of the keel, or the stern.
  • The keel sets the waterline. The bottom of your drawing rides where the yellow sub's does, so a taller sub grows upward and still clears the sea floor, and a long one keeps its distance from the slopes.
  • Glowing colors light themselves. Deep down there's no sunlight, and anything that doesn't glow fades to a shadow. The yellow sub's portholes glow so you can find it in the dark.
